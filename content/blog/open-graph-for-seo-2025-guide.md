# How to Use Open Graph for SEO: A 2025 Guide

Social meta tags don’t directly improve rankings—but they improve the things that do: click‑through rate, engagement, brand consistency, and shareability.

## Why social tags help SEO indirectly
- **Higher CTR** from compelling previews → more visits and engagement.
- **Brand consistency** builds recognition and trust.
- **Shareability** increases reach and the chance of earning links.

## Write tags that earn clicks
- **og:title (40–60 chars)**: Lead with the core benefit or outcome; include your keyword naturally.
- **og:description (90–160 chars)**: Start with value, add specifics, end with a soft CTA.
- **Image**: High‑contrast focal point, minimal overlay text, clear benefit. Use 1200 x 630.
- **Twitter**: Prefer `summary_large_image` for link shares.

Example:
```html
<meta property="og:title" content="Open Graph SEO: Turn Shares Into Clicks in 2025">
<meta property="og:description" content="Make your previews irresistible. Use proven titles, crisp images, and test before you post.">
<meta property="og:image" content="https://example.com/og-1200x630.jpg">
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">
```

## Advanced tips
- Version images (e.g., `image.jpg?v=2`) to bust caches after creative updates.
- Add UTM parameters to shared URLs to measure performance.
- Localize with `og:locale` and per‑market images.
- Create reusable preview templates to maintain brand consistency.

## QA checklist
- Every shareable page includes the “big four” OG tags.
- Titles and descriptions are unique per page.
- Images meet recommended size and aspect ratio.
- Previews are validated before publishing.
